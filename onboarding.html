Trust-first onboarding

From first application to verified support.

We keep the process simple and credible: clear application, proof, staff review, and a recommended service package. This protects both the client and the platform.

01

Create your profile

Tell us about the farm, enterprise, location, goals, and the service you need. Keep it practical and honest.

02

Upload evidence

Provide a government ID, business document, or farm evidence. Separate document consent must be given before files are accepted.

03

Staff verification

MoFirms reviews the application and checks that the details match the stated business or farm context.

04

Choose a package

Once approved, the client selects the advisory plan, onboarding package, or enterprise support option.

05

Pay and proceed

Payment is confirmed before work begins, and each engagement has a clear scope, timeline, and support plan.

Why this model works

Trust is the product.

MoFirms is not a quick-signup marketplace. It is a trusted advisory and referral platform for real farmers and enterprises. Verification reduces fraud, improves follow-through, and allows staff to focus on higher-value support.

  • Verified identity checks
  • Document consent and retention controls
  • No fake funding promises
  • Clear package pricing and scope
  • Staff-led review before work begins
