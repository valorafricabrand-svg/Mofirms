Pricing policy

Do plans guarantee funding?

No. MoFirms advises and prepares plans; it does not provide loans, investment guarantees, or automatic funding decisions.

Can I pay later?

Payment requests are currently limited to approved private-beta members using M-PESA STK Push. Contact MoFirms to discuss other arrangements before accepting a quote.

Is there a verification fee?

There is no standalone paid verification badge. Each package’s price and scope are shown before payment.
