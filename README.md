# MoFirms

Marks Organic Farms Concepts Limited, trading as MoFirms, was established in 2019. We help farmers, entrepreneurs and organisations plan agricultural enterprises, assess opportunities and grow responsibly, with financial analysis, market development and digital tools.

## Website (Stage one)

Static site: open index.html, or upload the folder to any host.
- Edit services, statuses, projects, portals at the top of js/app.js (change `s:"planned"` to `s:"now"` to relabel).
- Enquiry form opens the visitor's email app. For production, connect a server-side form handler with spam protection (brief, section 6).
- Before launch: confirm phone/WhatsApp consent, logo, domain, privacy notice wording, and company sign-off on all copy.
