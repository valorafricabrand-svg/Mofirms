Local pilot · synthetic data only

SmartHasibu

“Hasibu” is Kiswahili for account or reckoning. SmartHasibu is a phone-friendly bookkeeping pilot for farmers and small enterprises. Record sales and costs, separate farm activities, track unpaid credit, and review monthly totals.

The pilot runs locally and is not yet publicly launched. Use synthetic information only; do not enter real financial or personal details.

    A farmer-led association building its next steps

    Vihiga Organic Farmers Association (VOFA) is a separate, farmer-governed community-based organisation—not a MoFirms subsidiary. Its guide reports that VOFA has adopted its Constitution, completed CBO registration, opened a bank account, and begun farmer registration across Vihiga’s 29 wards.     Reported seedling progress: the guide says KALRO committed oil-palm seedlings for Emuhaya and that 500 of 1,000 discussed seedlings had been delivered to two members. VOFA noted this delivery had not yet been entered in its asset register.

    What needs to happen before a cluster can proceed

    1. Understand member readiness.Survey interested members’ production, capacity, and willingness to participate.
    2. Check demand and viability.Assess potential buyers, supply terms, costs, likely earnings after costs, working-capital needs, and environmental fit.
    3. Share a clear plan.Give interested members the proposed costs, responsibilities, risks, and terms to review.
    4. Obtain formal approval.VOFA’s Committee must approve a cluster before the association commits resources or confirms partners.

    With enough interested, verified members, a cluster could explore buyer negotiations, group input prices, and shared transport. Any approach to financiers depends on demonstrated production and genuine buyer interest; no outcome is guaranteed.

    Financial safeguards: the proposed mutual-guarantee fund is not an approved scheme; VOFA’s guide says no deposits are being requested. Carbon budgets assume zero income until eligibility, methodology, and net benefit are independently verified.

    About MoFirms

    Our approach starts with the enterprise: what it can produce, who will buy, what resources are needed and which risks must be managed. We help turn ideas into structured plans and identify the expertise, information and relationships needed to deliver them.

    Our advisory approach draws on decades of combined experience in agricultural finance, risk management and capacity building. Where an assignment needs specialist expertise, we coordinate with appropriately qualified professionals.

    Mission

    To help farmers and enterprises make informed decisions, improve their business practices and develop commercially sound, environmentally responsible projects.

    Vision

    Productive farms and resilient enterprises supported by dependable information, practical skills and access to markets.

    Customer portals

    Portals A and B support farmer, supplier, buyer, and service-provider registration. Portals C and D accept specialist financial-analysis and risk-and-opportunity assessment requests; staff review the scope and quote before work begins. Portal E is a separate, manually vetted research service; respondent participation is voluntary and researchers see aggregate results only. These services remain in local development and are not open for real personal, financial, or research data until secure production hosting and the privacy notice are approved.
