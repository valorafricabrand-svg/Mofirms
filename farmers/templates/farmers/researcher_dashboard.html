Research access rules

Access is limited to your own study proposals and safely aggregated results. You cannot browse registrants, respondent contacts, invitation links, or individual answers. MoFirms staff issue invitations only to customer contacts with a separate research-invitation opt-in. Respondents consent separately for each study.

Each study needs its own manual review, accepted fee quote, resolved ethics status, and staff deployment approval. The portal does not send emails or guarantee a sample size.
