Portal E · Researcher registration

Apply to conduct research with MoFirms

Research organisations, universities, marketing and insights companies, and students may apply independently of farmer or partner registration.

Vetting and access boundaries

  • Every applicant and every study is manually reviewed. Creating an account does not grant access to farmer or partner records.
  • MoFirms staff invite only registrant contacts who separately opted in to research invitations. Respondents make a separate, voluntary choice for each study.
  • Researchers see aggregate, de-identified results only. Individual responses, contact details, and respondent lists are not available to researchers.
  • Portal E questions support fixed answer choices only. Do not design questions that ask respondents to enter names, contact details, account data, health data, or other identifying information.
  • Use synthetic information only until MoFirms approves the privacy, ethics, vetting, retention, and production security arrangements.
