Register your business or services

Tell MoFirms what you supply, buy, or do; where you operate; and how you could work with farmers and agricultural businesses. You can select more than one partner role.

Before you begin

  • Give accurate information and provide evidence only when relevant to the specific claim being reviewed.
  • Business registration and identity evidence is optional. Do not upload tax PIN records, bank statements, passwords, unrelated people's IDs, or confidential customer records.
  • Uploads are limited to {{ max_documents }} documents, {{ max_file_size_mb }} MB each and {{ max_total_size_mb }} MB total. Accepted types: PDF, JPG and PNG.
  • Uploaded evidence is scheduled for deletion after one year. Partner profiles are scheduled for deletion after two years without recorded contact.
  • Submitting a profile does not make your company, identity, licence, products, or services “verified.” MoFirms staff review claims and record the evidence and scope of each decision.

This service is running in local development. Do not enter real personal or company information, or upload real documents, until MoFirms has approved the privacy notice and deployed the portal to secure production hosting.
