Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Marks Organic Farms Concepts Limited ("MoFirms", registered in Kenya, No. PVT-5JULR6L) handles personal data when you use mofirm.co.ke, the Smart Hasibu cashbook, and the MoFirms WhatsApp service. We process personal data in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019.

Unaweza kuomba kuona, kurekebisha au kufuta taarifa zako kwa kutuma barua pepe kwa info@mofirm.co.ke au ujumbe wa WhatsApp kwa +254 140 949 415.

Who to contact

Email info@mofirm.co.ke, call or WhatsApp +254 140 949 415.

What we collect

  • Farmer and partner registrations: name, phone, email, county and local area, preferred contact and language, farm or business details you choose to share, consent dates, and any supporting documents you upload.
  • WhatsApp service: your WhatsApp phone number, the messages you send to the MoFirms bot, and your registration answers. Your personal login code is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so it cannot be read by us or anyone else.
  • Smart Hasibu cashbook: your name, email, password (stored hashed) and the sales, costs and credit records you enter.
  • Enquiries, newsletter and service requests: the contact details and information you submit in consultation, newsletter, financial analysis, risk assessment and research forms.
  • Website assistant: questions you type into the "Ask MoFirms AI" chat. Please do not include personal details in these questions.

How we use it

  • To review and verify registrations, respond to enquiries, and provide the services you request.
  • To let you sign in and check your registration status on the website or on WhatsApp.
  • To send research survey invitations only if you separately opted in.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only authorised MoFirms staff can view registrations and documents. We use these service providers to run our services, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Render hosts our website and database.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) carries messages between you and the MoFirms WhatsApp service.
  • Google generates answers for the website assistant, and hosts the inbox that receives email sent to info@mofirm.co.ke.
  • Cloudflare provides our domain and email forwarding.
  • Brevo delivers our newsletter emails to subscribers. Newsletter drafts may be written with AI help (Google Gemini) from notes our team provides, and every issue is checked by our team before it is sent.

We may also disclose data where Kenyan law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded documents are deleted 1 year after upload.
  • Registrations, enquiries and service requests are deleted after 2 years without recorded contact with you.
  • Unfinished WhatsApp registrations are deleted after 90 days without activity.
  • Smart Hasibu records are kept while your account is active. Entries you delete are hidden from your cashbook; to have your cashbook permanently erased, ask us using the steps below.

How we protect it

Passwords and WhatsApp login codes are stored only in hashed form, uploaded documents are kept in private storage that only authorised staff can download, and access is limited to staff who need it to review your request.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or object to how we use it. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya).

How to delete your data

  1. Email info@mofirm.co.ke with the subject "Delete my data", or send "Delete my data" to MoFirms on WhatsApp at +254 140 949 415.
  2. Include the phone number or email you registered with, and your reference number if you have one.
  3. We will confirm the request comes from you, delete your registration, documents, WhatsApp record and Smart Hasibu data, and reply to confirm when it is done.

Smart Hasibu users can also delete individual entries and empty enterprises themselves at any time from their cashbook.
